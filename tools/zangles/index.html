--- layout: default title: Zangles ---

Enter any three values of a triangle, including at least one side, and Zangles will find the rest. Side a is opposite angle A, b is opposite B, and c is opposite C. Angles are in degrees. For a right triangle, set one angle to 90.

The drawing shows the values you entered in green and the box you're typing in in orange. Once there is enough to solve, it redraws to the real shape.
