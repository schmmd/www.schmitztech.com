## Games

I recently revived some of the games I made in early high school using AI so they are easily playable from the web.

* [Boxed](https://schmmd.github.io/boxes/). A [Beasts](https://classic-mac.fandom.com/wiki/Beast) clone.
* [Casino World](https://schmmd.github.io/casino-world/). The first game I wrote. Gamble to your heart's content!
* [Chair Equals Money](https://schmmd.github.io/chairequalsmoney/). A sokoban clone.
* [Keep of the Demon Lord](https://schmmd.github.io/keepofthedemonlord/). A mediocre (but nostalgic) role-playing game.
* [Schmitriz](https://schmmd.github.io/schmitriz/). A tetris clone.
* [Schnake](https://schmmd.github.io/schnake/). A snake clone.
