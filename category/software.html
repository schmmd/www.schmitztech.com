Games

I recently revived some of the games I made in early high school using AI so they are easily playable from the web.

  • Boxed. Cage the squirrels by pushing boxes around them. But beware: the squirrels bite. As the levels increase not only do the squirrels multiply, but they quicken and the boxes needed to confine them become scarce! Source.
  • Casino World. The first game I wrote for Windows. Gamble to your heart's content! Source.
  • Chair Equals Money. The official Schmitztech.com Chair Equals Money game! Push around the chairs and turn them into money in a Sokoban-like game. The simple rules only betray the complex strategy required. Source.
  • Keep of the Demon Lord. My first major project in Visual Basic. A mediocre (but nostalgic) role-playing game. Source.
  • Schmitriz. A straightforward, sleek, efficient, and robust Tetris clone. Additionally it features customizable background, customizable piece pictures, and a competitive two player mode. Without a doubt Schmitriz is my favorite Tetris, rivaled only by the originals. Source.
  • Schnake. Schnake is a snake/nibbles game. Eat to your hearts content, but be careful. As you eat, you gain length. Eventually, you need to twist your lengthy body into a tight structure to avoid crashing into yourself and rupturing your appetite. Source.
