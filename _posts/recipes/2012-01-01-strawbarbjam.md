* 2 c strawberries (mashed)
* 2 c rhubarb (chopped and mashed)
* 3/2 c honey
* 3 t pectin
* 3 t calcium solution

Great flavor, perfect amount of sweetness, but it did not set well. I would try 4t pectin and 4t calcium solution next time.
