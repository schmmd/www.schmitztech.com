Blue tea is a very dark tea that has berry and lemon flavors.

Ingredient proportions by weight:

* 10 parts rose hips (blender chopped)
* 10 parts dried blueberries (blender chopped)
* 10 parts elderberries (blender chopped)
* 9 parts butterfly pea flower
* 7 parts blackberry leaf
* 6 parts lemon peal
* 4 parts lemon balm
* 3 parts lemongrass
* 1 part calendula petals (or as much as you can gather)
