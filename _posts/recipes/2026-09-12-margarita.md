author: Michael Schmitz

tags: [cocktails]
---

In a shaker with ice, combine:

* 2 parts tequila (reposada or anejo)
* 1 part lime juice, fresh
* 1 part (or a little less) orange liqueur (Cointreau or Grand Marnier)

Shake and pour over ice.  Rim the glass with salt first if you like.
