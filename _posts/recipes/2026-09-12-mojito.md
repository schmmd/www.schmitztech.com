Per glass:

* 10 mint leaves
* 2 t sugar
* 1 lime
* 2 oz aged rum
* soda water

Muddle the mint gently with the sugar and halved lime in the bottom of the
glass.  Add the rum, mix, and strain.  Pour into a glass filled with ice, and
top with soda water.  Stir and garnish with a mint sprig.
