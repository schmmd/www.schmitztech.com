## Android App Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 10, 2026**

This is the privacy policy for the Android applications published on Google
Play by Schmitztech LLC ("we", "us"):

* WA Tides

Together these are referred to below as "the apps". This policy does not cover
the [schmitztech.com](https://schmitztech.com) website, which is a static
site with no accounts, forms, or analytics.

### The short version

The apps collect nothing about you. We run no servers and receive no data
from the apps, so there is nothing for us to store, share, or sell. The one
thing an app sends over the network is a request for public tide data, as
described below.

### Data the apps do not collect

The apps do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive
user data. In particular, they do not collect:

* names, email addresses, or other contact information
* location data
* device identifiers or advertising identifiers
* photos, files, contacts, calendar entries, or messages
* usage analytics, diagnostics, or crash reports
* purchase or financial information

### Data stored on your device

Anything you enter into an app, along with its settings, is stored locally on
your device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling an
app deletes it. We keep no copy and have no way to recover it.

If you back up your device, Android's own backup service may include app data
in that backup, under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). That is a
function of your device, not of the apps.

### Tide data

To show tide predictions, WA Tides requests published tide data from the
National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA). The request names the
tide station you are viewing. It carries no account, no identifier, and
nothing personal about you.

As with any request your device makes over the internet, NOAA can see the IP
address it came from; this is inherent to how the internet works and is not
something we collect or receive. NOAA handles that data under its own privacy
policy.

### No third-party services

The apps contain no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, crash reporters, or
tracking SDKs. No data is sold or shared with third parties, because none is
collected.

### Permissions

Each app requests only the permissions listed on its own Google Play store
page. Permissions are used solely to provide the feature they belong to, and
never to collect, profile, or transmit data about you.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an
updated effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the effective
date above.
